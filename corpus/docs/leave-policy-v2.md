## Annual leave

Every full-time employee accrues 24 days of paid annual leave per calendar year, credited monthly.

Requests for leave must reach the employee's manager no later than fourteen days before the absence begins.

## Carry-over

Unused annual leave may be carried over to the following year up to a maximum of 10 days.

Leave carried over beyond the cap is forfeited and cannot be encashed.

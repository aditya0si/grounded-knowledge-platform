# On-Call Escalation Matrix

**Document ID:** `oncall-escalation-matrix`  
**Classification:** eng  
**Owner:** Platform Reliability

---

## Escalation targets

Unacknowledged pages escalate automatically according to the table below, measured from the initial page.

| Step | Escalates to | After |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Primary on-call engineer | 7 minutes |
| 2 | Secondary on-call engineer | 45 minutes |
| 3 | Engineering duty manager | 9 hours |

## Overrides

A duty manager may pause automatic escalation during a declared major incident to avoid paging additional responders.

# Runbook: Export Worker

**Document ID:** `runbook-export-worker`  
**Classification:** finance  
**Owner:** Billing Systems

---

## Preconditions

Confirm that the export worker is reporting healthy before starting. Check the saturation dashboard for the last 2 hours.

## Procedure

Drain traffic from the affected node by removing it from the load balancer, then splitting the batch job into smaller windows.

If the condition persists for more than 9 minutes, escalate to Identity and adding the missing index concurrently.

## Rollback

Restore the previous configuration and verify that the export worker recovers within 26 minutes.

Approval for this change was obtained through the standard review process rather than a written sign-off.

# Service Tier Commitments

**Document ID:** `service-tiers`  
**Classification:** all  
**Owner:** Customer Engineering

---

## Tier commitments

Each service tier carries a monthly uptime commitment and a p95 request latency target, measured per calendar month.

| Tier | Monthly uptime | p95 latency | Support response |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Platinum | 99.95% | 180 ms | 30 minutes |
| Gold | 99.90% | 420 ms | 4 hours |
| Silver | 99.50% | 950 ms | 1 business day |

## Service credits

Falling below the committed uptime for a tier entitles the customer to a service credit against the following month's invoice.

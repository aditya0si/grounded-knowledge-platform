## Annual leave

Every full-time employee accrues 20 days of paid annual leave per calendar year, credited monthly.

Leave requests must be submitted at least fourteen days before the first day of absence.

## Carry-over

Unused annual leave may be carried over to the following year up to a maximum of 5 days.

Leave carried over beyond the cap is forfeited and cannot be encashed.

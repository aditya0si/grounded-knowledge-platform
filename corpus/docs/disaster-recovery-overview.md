**Classification:** eng  
**Owner:** Platform Reliability

## Objectives

Northwind Systems targets a recovery point objective of 22 minutes and a recovery time objective of 12 hours for tier one services.

These objectives describe our ambition, not a contractual commitment. Contractual commitments are recorded in the service tier documents.

## Scope

This overview does not describe incident handling, retention of incident records, or paging procedures. Those are governed by the incident response policy and the on-call escalation matrix.
